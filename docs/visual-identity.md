# Graphyard visual identity

For anyone placing the mark, and what colour may never mean.

The mark draws a lowercase **g** as a connected path, its three nodes independently owned work joining a delivery flow.

- **Assets, under `web/public/`:** `graphyard-symbol.svg` (pale green, dark backgrounds), `graphyard-symbol-ink.svg` (dark green, light backgrounds), `favicon.svg` (the simplified mark on a dark rounded square, for tabs and small sizes)
- **Usage:** self-contained SVGs under the repository's Apache-2.0 licence, edited by their path coordinates; keep clear space of one node diameter; never stretch or rotate the mark
- **Palette:** pale green `#c5e69b`, ink `#243923`, dark surface `#111714` — decorative, never a status indicator; status uses text and accessible UI semantics
