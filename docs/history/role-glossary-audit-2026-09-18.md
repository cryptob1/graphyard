# Role-glossary audit — September 18, 2026

For a maintainer editing the guides: why the role vocabulary is fixed.

The audit found seven recurring substitutions across the guides and resolved each into one canonical term: the **Canonical usage** and **Never** entries of [the glossary](../glossary.md#the-eight-distinctions); `tests/docs-glossary.test.ts` keeps them from drifting.
