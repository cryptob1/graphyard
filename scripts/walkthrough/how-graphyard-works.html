Graphyard · how it works

One loop, from a goal to production

You state what you want. One master loop breaks it into small work items, has AI agents build and review each one, tests it on this machine, merges it and ships it. People decide the goals. Every gate is Graphyard's own, and nothing depends on GitHub.

Who does what

Master loop

The one owner of the flow: dispatches, tests, gets the review, merges, ships, rolls back and watches itself for stalls.

Workers

Build one item each in their own worktree and submit a commit. They never touch the remote.

Reviewers

Judge each change against its criteria. Never the agent that built it.

Control plane

Holds every item, decision and result with full history.

What people decide

People keep exactly three decisions. Everything else is the loop's, and it never asks a person for a token, a repository or an approval.

  • Goals and priorities
  • Spending money or opening third-party accounts
  • Issuing credentials to people

When something goes wrong

The loop handles it in this order, and stops at the first that works:

  1. Retry the step.
  2. Roll the change or the release back.
  3. Show one line in the loop's status: what is stuck, since when, and what it tried.

If nothing has moved for too long, the loop's own stall watchdog raises it. Nothing becomes a work item unless there is code to change.
