Graphyard · how it works

One loop, from a goal to production

The master is an AI coordinator: it reasons and decides. The loop is a repeating program: it observes state and carries out routine actions. Workers implement. Graphyard owns leases, history and gates; Herdr runs sessions. A separate deploy supervisor serves main.

Who does what

Master AI

Reasons about goals and failures, files work and applies routine decisions under its operator-agent identity.

Loop program

Repeats observations, launches sessions and executes eligible routine actions. Completed cycles alone do not prove delivery.

Deploy supervisor

A separate service builds and serves main. It checks deployment health and records the serving commit.

Workers

Build one item each in their own worktree and submit a commit. They never touch the remote.

Reviewers

Judge each change against its criteria. Never the agent that built it.

Control plane

Owns leases, history and gates independently of Herdr. Only its merge writer pushes main.

What people decide

People keep exactly three decisions. The master applies every other decision. Review, proof and routine repair proceed automatically without new human approvals.

  • Goals and priorities
  • Spending money or opening third-party accounts
  • Issuing credentials to people

When something goes wrong

A failed trial on both main and the change is a base failure. The writer holds the change; the master diagnoses the failing check and files or advances repair work. Workers implement the repair with all gates intact.

The deploy supervisor can restore a failed deployment. Required app-case failures are recorded for recovery through the merge writer. A liveness watchdog only detects incomplete loop cycles; it cannot judge delivery progress.
